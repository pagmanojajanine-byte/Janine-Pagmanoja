Vision, Mission, Goals and Objectives

Vision

A leading and empowering research university in fisheries, agriculture, education, and technology in Southeast Asia by 2030.

Mission

To produce globally competitive and empowered graduates in fisheries, agriculture, education, maritime studies, arts, sciences, and technology, and to capacitate individuals and communities towards sufficiency for nation building

Goals

The Bachelor of Science and Information Technology aims to prepare students with broad, integrated IT knowledge thereby producing highly educated and innovative graduates who can bridge technology with other disciplines in their organization.

Objectives

    Specifically, the BSIT program aims to:
  • To produce globally competitive graduates adhering to the current IT industry standard;
  • Help students learn how to evaluate current and emerging technologies, and identify user needs; design user-friendly interface, apply, configure and manage technologies, and assess the impact of technologies on the individuals, organization and society.
  • Inculcate core life values and right attitude to continually uplift professionalism.
